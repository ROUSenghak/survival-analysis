\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Real BOAMP notices carry no field stating that one contract renews another, so
no direct measurement of linkage precision or recall is possible on them. This
report documents a synthetic benchmark built to answer the narrower question
that \emph{is} answerable: whether a candidate linkage method recovers
\emph{known} recurrence relations in a population whose observable structure has
been calibrated to BOAMP. A latent procurement world --- buyers, establishments,
recurring needs, contract cycles, and the successor relation between them --- is
generated first; BOAMP-like publication notices are then emitted from it and
subjected to identifier, name, CPV, duration, and text corruption whose rates are
estimated from the real corpus conditionally, not marginally. A sealed truth
layer is retained and never exposed to any linkage code.

The current central world contains \rvRowsObservedNotices\ observed notices
drawn from \rvRowsBuyers\ latent buyers and \rvRowsCycles\ contract cycles, with
\rvRowsCorruptionLog\ logged corruption events. Across \rvValidationMetricCount\
metrics in \rvValidationGateCount\ gates the validation framework reports
\code{\rvValidationStatus}, with \rvValidationMetricFailures\ metric-level
failures, all in non-critical gates. Every generated artifact
(\rvReplayReplicatesPassed/\rvReplayReplicatesChecked) reproduces bit-for-bit
from its recorded seeds and configuration.

The separate five-level readiness assessment is the operative statement of what
the benchmark may be used for, and it is deliberately conservative:
preliminary synthetic-only work is supported with limitations, while controlled
algorithm comparison, final algorithm ranking, and release as a validated
benchmark all currently fail. The binding reasons are seed-to-seed instability
of the difficulty metrics and unstable probe rankings, not defects in the
generator's internal consistency. Nothing in this report estimates real BOAMP
recurrence prevalence or real-world linkage accuracy, and the benchmark is not
a substitute for the real ground truth that does not exist.
\end{abstract}

\section{Executive summary}

\paragraph{What this is.} A controlled, fully reproducible synthetic corpus of
French public-procurement notices in which the answer to ``does notice $j$
continue the contract that notice $i$ announced?'' is known by construction, and
in which the observable record --- missing identifiers, drifting buyer names,
absent CPV codes, boilerplate text --- has been made to resemble the real BOAMP
corpus of \rvCorpusNotices\ notices published between \rvCorpusFirstPublication\
and \rvCorpusLastPublication.

\paragraph{Why it exists.} The production pipeline links a source contract to a
later notice when a weighted similarity score clears a frozen threshold. On real
BOAMP that produces \rvLayerOneLinks\ accepted links from \rvLayerOneSources\
eligible sources (\rvLayerOneLinkRate) --- but there is no way to say how many of
those are correct, because no legal-renewal field exists. Every quality number
the production pipeline can report is either model-based or conditional on its
own accepted links. The benchmark supplies the missing denominator, in a world
whose realism is a documented, measured claim rather than an assumption.

\paragraph{What the current run establishes.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Internal correctness.} All \rvGatesCritical\ critical gates that
  concern internal consistency, specification recovery, candidate environment,
  privacy, and algorithm utility pass. No truth column, alias, normalised truth
  value, or hash of a truth identifier reaches the observed table.
  \item \textbf{Observable realism, conditionally.} The candidate environment
  --- the property that decides whether a linker can find the successor at all
  --- now matches the real corpus closely: zero-candidate rate
  \rvCandNowZeroRate\ synthetic against \rvCandRealZeroRate\ real, a gap of
  \rvCandZeroAbsDiffPp\ percentage points.
  \item \textbf{Difficulty is non-trivial and measured.} Of
  \rvTruthPairsInScope\ true successor pairs inside the algorithm's own scope,
  the production \rvTemporalWindowMonths-month blocking window reaches
  \rvBlockingRecallProduction; widening to \rvWidenedProbeWindowMonths\ months reaches
  \rvBlockingRecallWidened. The best frozen probe linker attains pair
  $F_1 = \rvProbeBestFOne$ --- far from both 0 and 1, which is what makes the
  benchmark discriminative.
  \item \textbf{Reproducibility.} \rvReplayReplicatesPassed\ of
  \rvReplayReplicatesChecked\ generated artifacts replay to identical canonical
  content hashes.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What it does not establish.} Seed-to-seed spread is material:
production blocking completeness over \rvRobBlockingSeeds\ central seeds has
mean \rvRobBlockingMean\ and standard deviation \rvRobBlockingStd\
(coefficient of variation \rvRobBlockingCv), and probe rankings flip between
seeds. Consequently
\code{READY\_FOR\_CONTROLLED\_ALGORITHM\_COMPARISON},
\code{READY\_FOR\_FINAL\_ALGORITHM\_RANKING} and
\code{READY\_FOR\_VALIDATED\_SYNTHETIC\_BENCHMARK\_RELEASE} all read
\code{FAIL}. The benchmark is currently an instrument for debugging and
integrating linkage code, not for ranking linkage algorithms.

\section{Research objective and benchmark purpose}
\label{sec:objective}

The parent study models time to renewal of French public digital/ICT
procurement contracts. Its event is not observed: BOAMP publishes notices, not
contract lineages. The study therefore \emph{constructs} the event by record
linkage --- for each eligible source contract, later notices from the same buyer
near the estimated contract end are scored, and the best-scoring candidate is
accepted if it clears a threshold. Every survival conclusion inherits whatever
error that construction makes.

That error is unmeasurable on real data. The purpose of this benchmark is to
make it measurable in a controlled setting, and to state precisely how far a
measurement made there transfers. Formally, write $\mathcal{L}$ for a linkage
procedure, $\mathcal{R}$ for the real BOAMP corpus, $\mathcal{S}$ for a
synthetic corpus, and $T$ for the true successor relation. On $\mathcal{R}$,
$T$ is unobserved and no estimate of $\Pr(\hat{T} = T)$ exists. On
$\mathcal{S}$, $T$ is known by construction, so precision, recall, and
cluster-level accuracy are computable exactly. The transfer claim is
conditional and is the only one this report makes:

\begin{quote}\itshape
If $\mathcal{S}$ reproduces the observable properties of $\mathcal{R}$ that
materially affect $\mathcal{L}$, then the behaviour of $\mathcal{L}$ on
$\mathcal{S}$, evaluated across a documented envelope of scenario assumptions,
is useful calibration and comparison evidence about its behaviour on
$\mathcal{R}$.
\end{quote}

The design follows the gold-standard-and-corruption framework of
\citet{lam2024synthetic}, adapted from personal identifiers to procurement
recurrence: generate a clean population with known links, then corrupt the
observable layer with mechanisms whose rates are calibrated to the target
corpus, and evaluate against the retained truth. The linkage vocabulary
--- match, potential match, non-match, blocking, pairs completeness, reduction
ratio --- follows \citet{fellegi1969theory} and \citet{christen2012data}, and
the design of evaluating linkage software on constructed data follows
\citet{ferrante2012transparent}.

\section{Scope and limitations}

\paragraph{In scope.} A single geographic and sectoral slice: Pays de la Loire
buyers, digital/ICT contracts, publication dates \rvObservationStart\ to
\rvObservationEnd. One central scenario plus bracketing scenarios. Notice
types are reduced to calls (\code{APPEL\_OFFRE}) and awards
(\code{ATTRIBUTION}); the residual \code{OTHER} category of the real corpus
(\rvCorpusOtherShare\ of notices) is not generated.

\paragraph{Out of scope, and never claimed.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Real recurrence prevalence.} The generator's recurrence
  propensity is an assumption, not an estimate. See
  Section~\ref{sec:provenance}.
  \item \textbf{Real linkage accuracy.} Every precision and recall number in
  this report is measured against generated truth.
  \item \textbf{Threshold optimality.} The production threshold is an output of
  a particular pipeline; it is reported for comparison and never used to define
  truth.
  \item \textbf{National generalisation.} The calibration corpus is one region.
  \item \textbf{Held-out validation.} No real-BOAMP partition was withheld from
  generator design, so observable-fidelity results are in-sample with respect
  to calibration.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The original BOAMP data}

\paragraph{Source and extraction.} Monthly JSON extracts from the
\emph{Bulletin officiel des annonces des march\'es publics} were retrieved via
the Opendatasoft Explore API, filtered server-side to Pays de la Loire, for
publication months \rvObservationStart\ through \rvObservationEnd. After
flattening and de-duplication on the BOAMP notice identifier, the prepared
corpus contains \rvCorpusNotices\ unique notices with publication dates from
\rvCorpusFirstPublication\ to \rvCorpusLastPublication.

\paragraph{Schemas.} Two incompatible record schemas coexist. The legacy BOAMP
format accounts for \rvCorpusLegacyShare\ of notices; the EU eForms format,
mandated from late 2023 and adopted gradually from 2024, accounts for
\rvCorpusEformsShare. The two differ in which fields exist at all, which is why
every missingness rate in this report is reported conditionally on schema and
never only marginally.

\paragraph{Notice types.} Normalised notice type splits
\rvCorpusCallShare\ calls, \rvCorpusAwardShare\ awards, and
\rvCorpusOtherShare\ other (modifications, cancellations, corrections).

\paragraph{Analysis population.} Applying the digital/ICT scope --- CPV
divisions \rvDigitalCpvDivisions, or a French-language keyword match on the cleaned
contract object --- to call notices yields \rvLayerOneSources\ eligible source
contracts, the population every production and benchmark comparison uses.

\section{Analysis population and preprocessing}

Preprocessing is layer-neutral and runs once
(\file{notebooks/01_data_engineering_pipeline.ipynb}, Part~C). It performs,
in order: de-duplication on the notice identifier; notice-type normalisation;
schema-family detection; CPV extraction across both schemas, including the
UBL commodity-classification paths eForms uses; buyer-identifier extraction and
validation; buyer-name normalisation; text cleaning; and duration extraction
with a plausibility filter of \rvDurationMinMonths--\rvDurationMaxMonths\ months.

Two derived quantities carry disproportionate weight and are flagged here
rather than buried.

\paragraph{Buyer key.} Buyer identity is resolved by a strict priority ladder:
a checksum-valid SIRET, else a checksum-valid SIREN, else the normalised buyer
name. Only \rvCalibSiretPresentRate\ of notices carry a checksum-valid SIRET, so
the large majority of buyer identities rest on a normalised string. This is the
single largest source of both false splits and false merges, and the benchmark
models both explicitly.

\paragraph{Estimated contract end.} Only \rvCalibDurationPresentRate\ of notices
declare a duration at all. Missing durations on eligible sources are imputed by
the median observed duration of the same CPV division. The imputed duration then
determines the estimated end date, which determines the temporal blocking
window, the temporal score component, and the survival covariate --- a
dependency chain documented as the pipeline's highest-leverage assumption.

\section{Calibration methodology}
\label{sec:calibration}

\paragraph{Principle.} A quantity may be used to \emph{set} a generator
parameter only if it is measurable from raw or layer-neutral prepared fields,
without reference to any accepted link, score, or threshold. Quantities visible
only through accepted links are silver-standard: they may be reported and used
as validation targets, never as generator inputs. Quantities not measurable at
all become scenario assumptions and must be varied.

\paragraph{Estimator conventions.} Throughout, $\widehat{p}$ denotes an
empirical proportion computed on a stated population, and $p$ a model
probability the generator draws from. For a binary outcome $Y$ observed on cells
$c$ defined by conditioning variables, the generator consumes a
beta-binomially smoothed rate rather than the raw cell proportion:
\begin{equation}
\widehat{p}_c \;=\; \frac{\sum_{i \in c} Y_i \;+\; \kappa\, \widehat{p}_{\bullet}}
                        {n_c \;+\; \kappa},
\qquad
\widehat{p}_{\bullet} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_i ,
\label{eq:smoothing}
\end{equation}
with prior strength $\kappa = \rvSmoothingPriorStrength$ notices and
$\widehat{p}_{\bullet}$ the global rate. Shrinkage matters because the conditioning grid
(schema $\times$ year $\times$ notice type) contains cells with a handful of
notices, whose raw proportions are 0 or 1 and would otherwise be copied into the
generator as certainties.

\paragraph{Distances, not tests.} Agreement between a real and a synthetic
distribution is judged by effect size, never by a $p$-value. On
\rvCorpusNotices\ real notices any non-zero difference is statistically
significant, so significance carries no information about whether the benchmark
is fit for purpose. For a categorical variable with real distribution $P$ and
synthetic distribution $Q$ over categories $\mathcal{K}$ the report uses total
variation and Jensen--Shannon distance \citep{lin1991divergence}:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{TV}(P,Q) = \tfrac{1}{2}\sum_{k \in \mathcal{K}} |P_k - Q_k|,
\qquad
\mathrm{JS}(P,Q) = \tfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{KL}\!\left(P \,\|\, M\right)
                 + \tfrac{1}{2}\mathrm{KL}\!\left(Q \,\|\, M\right),
\quad M = \tfrac{1}{2}(P+Q).
\label{eq:tvjs}
\end{equation}
Both are bounded in $[0,1]$, require no distributional assumption, and are
interpretable as ``the largest probability mass the two distributions disagree
about'' and ``an information-theoretic divergence, symmetrised and bounded''
respectively. For continuous variables the report uses a scale-free first
Wasserstein distance, $W_1$ divided by the real interquartile range, so a
duration discrepancy and a text-length discrepancy are comparable. For a
conditional rate the report uses the count-weighted mean absolute error over
cells,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{WMAE} \;=\; \frac{\sum_c n_c \,\bigl|\widehat{p}^{\,\mathrm{syn}}_c - \widehat{p}^{\,\mathrm{real}}_c\bigr|}
                          {\sum_c n_c},
\label{eq:wmae}
\end{equation}
expressed in percentage points, which prevents a near-empty cell from dominating
the verdict.

\paragraph{Equivalence, not non-rejection.} Where a cluster bootstrap is
available (\rvValidationBootstrapReps\ replicates, resampling buyers rather than
notices so within-buyer dependence is respected), a metric is declared
equivalent only if the whole bootstrap interval lies inside the tolerance band,
following the two-one-sided-tests logic of
\citet{lakens2017equivalence,lakens2018equivalence}. Metrics without an interval
can reach at most ``not obviously discrepant''. This distinction is preserved in
the metric table and is why a gate can read \code{INCONCLUSIVE} rather than
\code{PASS}.

\section{Parameter-provenance framework}
\label{sec:provenance}

Every generator parameter and every validation rule belongs to exactly one
provenance class, recorded machine-readably in
\file{registries/parameter_registry.csv} with \rvRegistryRows\ rows. The
registry records, per row: the mathematical definition, the value or
distribution, the scenario-file value, the effective value actually used, the
estimation population, the estimating code, the uncertainty, whether the row may
be used for calibration, whether it must remain held out, and its known
limitations.

\paragraph{Why marginals are not enough.} Three of these quantities are
strongly conditional, and a generator matching only the marginal would model the
wrong mechanism.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Identifier availability} shows a regime shift centred on 2022
  that is independent of the schema cutover: 2022 and 2023 are both entirely
  legacy-schema in this corpus, yet SIRET presence jumps from roughly one notice
  in ten before 2022 to more than half in 2022--2023, then partially recedes.
  Figure~\ref{fig:idfidelity} shows the real curve and the generator's fit.
  \item \textbf{CPV missingness} is essentially a schema property: eForms
  mandates the field, legacy does not, and within legacy, calls omit CPV about
  three times as often as awards.
  \item \textbf{Duration presence} is near-deterministic in schema: essentially
  zero for every year through 2023, then substantial once eForms is adopted.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Buyer activity.} Notices per buyer are extremely concentrated --- a
heavy tail in which a small number of buyers account for most publications.
Figure~\ref{fig:buyeractivity} gives the rank-frequency and Lorenz views. This
matters for linkage because candidate density is a function of buyer activity: a
linker's hardest cases are concentrated in the tail.

\paragraph{Candidate environment.} Applying the production blocker to the real
corpus produces a median of \rvCandRealMedian\ candidate per source and a mean
of \rvCandRealMean, with \rvCandRealZeroRate\ of eligible sources receiving no
candidate at all. That zero-candidate rate is structural censoring: those
sources cannot be linked by any scoring rule, and reproducing it is a
prerequisite for the benchmark to be informative about blocking.

\section{Synthetic benchmark design}

\subsection{Two layers, and why the separation is load-bearing}

The generator emits two disjoint sets of tables
(Table~\ref{tab:units}). The \emph{observed layer} is a single BOAMP-like notice
table with \rvObservedColumns\ columns, containing only fields a linkage method
could read. The \emph{truth layer} carries the latent entities, the cycle
chains, the successor relation, per-notice pre-corruption values, and a complete
corruption log --- \rvTruthOnlyColumns\ truth-only columns across
\rvBenchmarkTables\ tables in total.

The separation is what makes exact blocking recall, recovery by corruption type,
and cluster-level accuracy computable. It is also why leakage is treated as a
release-blocking failure: a single truth column reaching the observed table
would turn every accuracy number downstream into an artifact. The leakage check
is not a column-name blocklist alone; it also rejects unsuffixed hidden-truth
aliases, exact and normalised truth-identifier \emph{values}, and MD5, SHA-1 and
SHA-256 hashes of truth identifiers.

\subsection{The scoped-candidate block}
\label{sec:scoped}

Version 0.2 of the generator produced a technically valid world that was
useless as a linkage benchmark: \rvCandPrevZeroRate\ of its digital-scope
sources had no candidate at all, against \rvCandRealZeroRate\ in the real
corpus. The cause was not corruption but timing --- true successor gaps drawn
from a distribution centred well outside the production window, combined with
too few same-buyer distinct needs to create distractors.

Version 0.3 adds a \emph{scoped-candidate block} that acts only on needs whose
CPV division falls in the digital scope. It has five tuned parameters, all
recorded as fidelity-tuned rather than empirical: a recurrence-propensity
multiplier of \rvScopedMultiplier, a near-window share of
\rvScopedNearWindowShare, a hard-negative chain-alignment rate of
\rvScopedHardNegativeRate, a scoped-buyer affinity share of
\rvScopedAffinityShare, and a high scoped-need probability of
\rvScopedNeedProbabilityHigh. Section~\ref{sec:cand} reports what they achieved.

Two properties of this block are worth stating explicitly, because they bound
what it can be accused of. It never assigns per-source candidate counts, scores,
accepted links, or thresholds --- it acts on latent recurrence timing and on how
needs cluster within a buyer, and candidate counts remain a \emph{post-hoc}
fidelity target. And chain alignment translates an entire distinct-need chain by
one constant offset, so $\text{target start} - \text{source expected end}$ is
unchanged by construction: the step cannot move the true-gap distribution at
all.

\subsection{Step 1 --- buyers}

Activity weights are drawn from a Pareto variate and normalised, so
concentration is emergent rather than assigned:
\begin{equation}
r_b \sim \mathrm{Pareto}(\alpha) + \delta,
\qquad
a_b = \frac{r_b}{\sum_{b'} r_{b'}},
\qquad \alpha = \rvParetoShape,\ \delta = \rvParetoOffset .
\end{equation}
Here $\mathrm{Pareto}(\alpha)$ is NumPy's parameterisation, whose support is
$[0,\infty)$ with density $\alpha(1+x)^{-(\alpha+1)}$ --- the Lomax form, not
the Pareto Type~I form assumed by the textbook identity
$\mathrm{Gini} = 1/(2\alpha-1)$. That identity is asymptotic and requires
$\alpha > 1$; at $B = \rvTargetNBuyers$ it is a poor guide, so $\alpha$ was set
by simulating the emergent Gini rather than by inverting the formula. The offset
$\delta$ keeps every weight strictly positive before normalisation and is an
implementation constant.

Departments are assigned by a deficit-filling pass over buyers in descending
activity, which makes the \emph{notice-weighted} department distribution track
the empirical one --- a per-buyer i.i.d.\ draw would let one high-activity buyer
distort it. Buyer type, name, alias propensity $\sim \mathrm{Beta}(2,5)$, and
identifier-quality propensity $\pi_b \sim \mathrm{Beta}(5,2)$ complete the
record. Names are syllable-generated; no real organisation name is ever copied.

\subsection{Step 2 --- establishments}

Each buyer receives one to three establishments, more likely several for
high-activity buyers. SIRENs are rejection-sampled until they pass the same Luhn
validation the production pipeline applies to real identifiers; each SIRET is
its buyer's SIREN concatenated with a zero-padded establishment sequence and the
unique valid check digit. ``Valid'' therefore means the same thing on both sides
of the comparison, and same-SIREN/different-SIRET ambiguity exists structurally
for the corruption step to exploit.

\subsection{Step 3 --- needs}

The number of needs per buyer is Poisson with a mean allocated continuously by
activity weight rather than by activity tier:
\begin{equation}
N_b \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(\lambda_b),
\qquad
\lambda_b = f + a_b\bigl(\Lambda - fB\bigr),
\qquad
\Lambda = \sum_{b} \lambda^{\mathrm{tier}}_{b},
\end{equation}
where $f$ is a small floor keeping low-activity buyers viable and $\Lambda$ is
the total scale implied by the tier means. Collapsing this to three tier means
--- the earlier design --- cut the needs-per-buyer Gini from roughly $0.8$ to
$0.34$ and destroyed the dense candidate neighbourhoods that make linkage hard.

Each need draws a CPV division (from the empirical division distribution, or
from the scoped mixture below), a synthetic eight-digit CPV code, a
division-conditioned concept and vocabulary set, a duration profile
\begin{equation}
\mu_d \sim \mathrm{LogNormal}\bigl(\log 12,\ 0.7^2\bigr)
\ \text{ clipped to } [\rvDurationMinMonths,\rvDurationMaxMonths] \text{ months},
\end{equation}
and a recurrence propensity jittered multiplicatively around the scenario
central value $\rho_0 = \rvBaseRecurrencePropensity$:
\begin{equation}
\rho_d = \min\bigl(\rvRecurrencePropensityCap,\ \rho_0 \cdot J_d\bigr),
\qquad
J_d = 0.6\,\mathrm{Beta}(4,4) + 0.7 \in [0.7, 1.3].
\end{equation}
No need is ever given the scenario value exactly; heterogeneity in recurrence is
part of the design rather than noise added afterwards.

When the scoped block is active, the division draw becomes a two-component
mixture. A share \rvScopedAffinityShare\ of buyers are marked as
digital-affinity buyers; their needs fall in the scoped divisions with
probability \rvScopedNeedProbabilityHigh, and every other buyer's needs do so
with the residual probability that preserves the overall scoped mass. This
clusters digital needs within buyers --- which is what generates same-buyer
distinct-need distractors --- without changing the aggregate CPV composition.

\subsection{Step 4 --- cycles, and the censoring that shapes the truth}

For each need, cycle 1 starts uniformly inside its buyer's active window. Each
cycle draws a true duration around the need's profile,
\begin{equation}
D^{\ast}_{dk} \sim \mathcal{N}\bigl(\mu_d,\ (0.25\mu_d)^2\bigr)
\ \text{ clipped to } [\rvDurationMinMonths,\rvDurationMaxMonths],
\qquad
E^{\ast}_{dk} = S^{\ast}_{dk} + \rvMonthDays \cdot D^{\ast}_{dk} \text{ days}.
\end{equation}
A successor is realised only if a Bernoulli draw succeeds \emph{and} the
resulting start falls inside the observation window:
\begin{equation}
\Pr(\text{successor at } k{+}1) = \min\!\bigl(\rvRecurrencePropensityCap,\ \rho_d \cdot \gamma_d\bigr),
\qquad
\gamma_d = \begin{cases}\rvScopedMultiplier & d \text{ scoped},\\ 1 & \text{otherwise},\end{cases}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
S^{\ast}_{d,k+1} = E^{\ast}_{dk} + \rvMonthDays\, G_{dk},
\qquad
\text{realised only if } S^{\ast}_{d,k+1} \le \text{\rvObservationEnd},
\qquad K_d \le \rvMaxCyclesPerNeed .
\end{equation}
The gap is a two-component mixture: with probability \rvScopedNearWindowShare\
for a scoped need it is drawn from a near-window component, otherwise from the
scenario base component:
\begin{equation}
G_{dk} \sim
\begin{cases}
\mathcal{N}(\rvNearGapMeanMonths,\ \rvNearGapSdMonths^2)
  \text{ clipped to } [\rvNearGapMinMonths, \rvNearGapMaxMonths], & \text{scoped, w.p. } \rvScopedNearWindowShare,\\[3pt]
\mathcal{N}(\rvGapMeanMonths,\ \rvGapSdMonths^2)
  \text{ clipped below at } \rvGapMinMonths, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\label{eq:gap}
\end{equation}
Note the implemented form: these are \emph{clipped} normals, not renormalised
truncated normals. Mass falling outside the bounds is placed on the bounds
rather than redistributed, so the realised law has atoms at
$\rvGapMinMonths$ and at $\rvNearGapMinMonths$ and $\rvNearGapMaxMonths$.
Writing $\mathcal{N}$ ``truncated'' in the textbook sense would misdescribe the
code.

\paragraph{The censoring is not a detail.} Because a chain stops when
$E^{\ast}_{dk} + G_{dk}$ leaves the window, the gaps that \emph{reach} the truth
table are systematically shorter than the declared law of
Equation~\eqref{eq:gap}. Any recovery check that simulates the unconditional
draw will therefore fail for reasons that have nothing to do with the generator.
The specification-recovery gate censors each simulated gap by the headroom
remaining to its own source cycle, measured from $E^{\ast}_{dk}$ --- not from
$S^{\ast}_{dk}$, which was an earlier error that overstated headroom by one
cycle duration and biased the reference interval upward. The realised mean true
gap is \rvMeanTrueGapMonths\ months and lies inside the corrected 99\% Monte
Carlo interval.

\subsection{Step 5 --- the successor relation}

The truth table is a pure function of the realised cycle chains: cycle $k$ of
need $d$ emits \code{NEXT\_CYCLE} to cycle $k{+}1$ if that row exists, and
\code{NO\_SUCCESSOR} otherwise, with
$G_{dk} = (S^{\ast}_{d,k+1} - E^{\ast}_{dk})/\rvMonthDays$ recorded in months.
Nothing is hidden in this step: the three distinct reasons a chain can stop ---
failed Bernoulli draw, cycle cap, window boundary --- are all recorded as the
same \code{NO\_SUCCESSOR}, exactly as an analyst of real data would see them.

\subsection{Steps 6--7 --- notices and clean values}

Each cycle emits a call notice at $S^{\ast}_{dk}$, and with probability
\rvCalibAwardProbability\ an award notice after a delay
$\sim \mathcal{N}(\mathrm{\rvAwardDelayMeanDays}, \mathrm{\rvAwardDelaySdDays}^2)$ days clipped to
$[\mathrm{\rvAwardDelayMinDays}, \mathrm{\rvAwardDelayMaxDays}]$. The award delay is an
acknowledged design assumption: no observable call-to-award distribution was
carried into the calibration file.

Schema family is drawn probabilistically, not by a date cutoff: every notice
before \rvEformsAdoptionFirstYear\ is legacy, and from that year a notice is eForms
with the empirically observed adoption share for its year. An earlier hard cutoff at the mandate date
gave every post-mandate notice a 100\% eForms label when the real share never
exceeds \rvEformsAdoptionMaxShare, which then propagated into the schema-conditional duration
and CPV mechanisms.

Contract text is rendered from templates over the need's concepts and
vocabulary, with three length regimes so the length distribution has a tail. A
call and its award share the same underlying text, subject to light
publication-level variation at severity \rvTextSameCycleVariation --- the real
corpus's within-family similarity is essentially $1$, so this is deliberately
small. Successor-cycle lexical drift is a separate mechanism at severity
\rvTextNextCycleDrift, and is a scenario parameter because no empirical anchor
for true recurrence drift exists anywhere in the corpus.

\subsection{Step 8 --- observation and corruption}

Every field's corruption severity is scaled by one latent per-notice
record-quality class, so defects co-occur on the same notices rather than being
sampled independently per field --- the core corruption-design principle of
\citet{lam2024synthetic}. The class is drawn per notice from the scenario mix
(\rvQualityShareHigh\ high, \rvQualityShareMedium\ medium,
\rvQualityShareLow\ low, before tilting), tilted by the buyer's
identifier-quality propensity $\pi_b$:
\begin{equation}
\Pr(Q_\nu = \textsc{high}) \propto \phi_{\textsc{high}}\,(0.5 + \pi_b),
\qquad
\Pr(Q_\nu = \textsc{low}) \propto \phi_{\textsc{low}}\,(1.5 - \pi_b).
\end{equation}

The severity multiplier over (high, medium, low) is
$m(Q) = (\rvSeverityMultipliers)$ for identifiers, CPV, text and linked-notice
visibility. Duration has its own, much flatter spread
$(\rvDurationSeverityMultipliers)$. That is not a tuning convenience: with the shared
multiplier it is structurally impossible to reach the real corpus's duration
presence rate, because the high-quality class alone contributes more
duration-present notices than the entire real target allows. The data are saying
that duration is omitted near-uniformly across BOAMP regardless of overall
record quality --- an editorial convention --- while identifiers, CPV and text
do vary with record quality.

Field mechanisms, in the order applied:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Identifiers.} SIRET presence is drawn from the smoothed
  conditional rate $\widehat{p}_c$ of Equation~\eqref{eq:smoothing} on cells
  (schema, year, notice type). When present, a small share is replaced by a
  sibling establishment's SIRET under the same SIREN --- a valid identifier
  pointing at the wrong establishment, which no checksum can detect. When
  absent, the mass splits into SIREN-only, invalid-checksum, and both-missing
  outcomes.
  \item \textbf{Buyer name.} Either a generic-collision replacement drawn from a
  type-conditioned pool, or a name variant produced by one of six
  transformations (abbreviation, suffix removal, punctuation and accent
  flattening, service qualifier, geographic qualifier, token reorder). Rates are
  the empirical false-split and false-merge rates, scaled by the notice's
  severity and the buyer's alias propensity. Name-comparison behaviour of this
  kind is the classic string-similarity setting of
  \citet{jaro1989advances,cohen2003comparison}.
  \item \textbf{CPV.} A schema-and-type conditional missing rate, plus
  parent-code replacement, division-only truncation, generic-code substitution,
  and wrong-related-code substitution.
  \item \textbf{Duration.} Presence from the smoothed conditional rate; when
  present, the visible value is drawn from the empirical distribution of valid
  raw BOAMP durations by a deterministic low-discrepancy inverse-CDF map keyed
  on the notice identifier. The latent cycle length is \emph{not} rounded into
  the observed field --- observed duration and true duration are separate
  quantities, which is the point.
  \item \textbf{Text.} Exact cross-buyer template substitution, near-boilerplate
  with row-specific tokens, or generic weak procurement wording, at a rate
  conditional on notice type and buyer activity tier.
  \item \textbf{Linked-notice visibility.} An award's reference back to its call
  is dropped at the empirical unresolved-award rate.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Missingness terminology.} In the taxonomy of
\citet{rubin1976inference} and \citet{carpenter2021missing}, none of these
mechanisms is missing completely at random. Identifier, CPV and duration
presence are missing at random conditional on observed schema, year and notice
type; text degradation is additionally conditional on buyer activity tier. The
shared quality class $Q_\nu$ induces dependence \emph{between} fields, so the
joint missingness pattern is not the product of its marginals --- which is
exactly the property a linkage benchmark must have and an independent-Bernoulli
design would lose.

\paragraph{Identity persistence.} Observed buyer identity is drawn once per
notice family and reused across its members, and --- under the scoped block ---
across the cycles of one hidden need. This is a labelled assumption, not an
oversight: without it, sibling notices of one procurement episode drift apart in
buyer key and the benchmark becomes unreachable under production-style
buyer-key blocking. It is recorded in the mechanism trade-off log as an explicit
reachability-versus-conditional-fidelity trade.

\section{Reproducibility and random-seed management}
\label{sec:seeds}

The central world is generated from world seed \code{\rvWorldSeed} and
corruption seed \code{\rvCorruptionSeed}, with $B = \rvTargetNBuyers$ buyers.
Replicates vary only the world seed; the corruption seed follows it
deterministically. Every artifact directory carries a
\file{generation_metadata.json} recording both seeds, the resolved scenario and
defaults, SHA-256 hashes of every configuration file and of the calibration
corpus, row counts, the Git commit, and --- new in this revision --- the exact
numeric-library versions.

\paragraph{Why the environment is recorded.} Replay compares canonical table
\emph{content}, not Parquet bytes, so writer-version differences are already
tolerated. It does not tolerate a different NumPy: the generator and reduction
kernels are bit-stable per build, not across builds, so a different NumPy
reproduces every count and identity while differing in the last unit in the last
place of float columns --- enough to change a SHA-256. This was observed in
practice during the audit that produced this revision. The fix is twofold:
\file{requirements-lock.txt} pins the exact environment, and the replay
comparison now reports environment drift explicitly so the next such mismatch is
diagnosable instead of looking like generator drift. The current artifacts were
generated under Python \rvEnvPython, NumPy \rvEnvNumpy, pandas \rvEnvPandas,
PyArrow \rvEnvPyarrow.

\paragraph{Current replay status.} Canonical replay of the central world:
\code{\rvReplayStatus} across \rvReplayTables\ tables, environment drift
\rvReplayEnvironmentDrift. All-artifact replay:
\code{\rvReplayReplicatesStatus},
\rvReplayReplicatesPassed/\rvReplayReplicatesChecked.

\section{Hidden ground-truth structure}

\file{true_relations.parquet} holds one row per cycle: \code{NEXT\_CYCLE} with
a target and a true gap, or \code{NO\_SUCCESSOR}. A cycle is observed through
its call notice, so a cycle-level successor relation becomes a notice-level true
match between two call notices. Of the resulting true matches,
\rvTruthPairsInScope\ have both notices inside the algorithm's own
candidate-generation scope.

That scoping is deliberate and is reported separately rather than folded into
recall. A successor whose two notices are not both in scope cannot be found by
any method operating on those candidates; charging every probe for the same
fixed scoping loss would hide the differences between them, which is the only
thing the benchmark exists to reveal.

\section{Validation methodology}

The framework evaluates \rvValidationMetricCount\ metrics grouped into
\rvValidationGateCount\ gates, of which \rvGatesCritical\ are critical. A
critical gate fails the release on its own; a non-critical gate can only
downgrade the run to \code{PASS\_WITH\_WARNINGS}. This asymmetry is intentional:
the benchmark is allowed to simplify the real corpus provided the simplification
is documented in the fidelity budget, but anything that would silently change a
linkage conclusion, leak truth, or copy a real record is critical.

\paragraph{Consequence, and why the headline is not the whole story.} A
non-critical gate reports \code{WARNING} even when individual metrics inside it
failed. The run therefore reports the metric-level failure count explicitly:
\rvValidationMetricFailures\ failures, \rvValidationNoncriticalFailures\ of them
inside non-critical gates. Reading only \code{\rvValidationStatus} would
understate what the benchmark is carrying.

\paragraph{Diagnostics and what each is for.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{TV and JS} (Eq.~\ref{eq:tvjs}) for categorical composition:
  bounded, assumption-free, and interpretable as disagreement mass. Limitation:
  insensitive to \emph{which} categories disagree, so they are always read
  alongside the per-cell table.
  \item \textbf{Scaled $W_1$} for continuous fields: sensitive to location and
  spread, expressed in interquartile-range units so different fields are
  comparable. Limitation: one number cannot distinguish a shifted distribution
  from a differently-shaped one, which is why the duration comparison is also
  shown as a quantile plot (Figure~\ref{fig:qq}).
  \item \textbf{Weighted MAE over cells} (Eq.~\ref{eq:wmae}) for conditional
  rates: the correct object when the mechanism, not the average, is what must
  match. Limitation: it averages, so a large error in one important cell can be
  masked; the cell-level tables are retained.
  \item \textbf{Pairs completeness, pairs quality, reduction ratio} for
  blocking: the standard triple \citep{christen2012data}. Pairs completeness is
  recall of true matches into the candidate set; pairs quality is the share of
  candidates that are true matches; reduction ratio is how much of the quadratic
  comparison space was avoided.
  \item \textbf{Overlap coefficient} between the score distributions of true
  matches and hard negatives: the benchmark must be neither trivially separable
  nor hopeless, so this metric is gated on \emph{both} sides.
  \item \textbf{Cluster bootstrap} resampling buyers for interval estimates,
  because notices within a buyer are not independent.
  \item \textbf{Nearest-neighbour text overlap} against a real baseline for
  privacy: the synthetic corpus must not be closer to real text than real text
  is to itself.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Four things that are not the same.} \emph{Statistical significance}
is uninformative at this sample size and is not used. \emph{Practical
discrepancy} is the effect size against a pre-declared tolerance.
\emph{Observable fidelity} is agreement on what a linker can see.
\emph{Structural plausibility} is whether the latent mechanism is defensible ---
which fidelity cannot establish, since many recurrence processes yield similar
observed tables. \emph{Suitability for linkage evaluation} is a further step
again, and is what the readiness assessment, not the validation status, decides.

\section{Linkage-difficulty analysis}
\label{sec:difficulty}

\paragraph{Blocking dominates.} Of \rvTruthPairsInScope\ in-scope true successor
pairs, the production window recovers \rvBlockingRecallProduction\ and the
widened \rvWidenedProbeWindowMonths-month window recovers \rvBlockingRecallWidened. The gap is a property
of that window against these gaps, not a generator defect: the gap distribution
is deliberately centred outside the production window
(Equation~\ref{eq:gap}), because a benchmark whose true successors all fell
inside the window would report a blocking recall that says nothing about renewal
timing.

  \caption{True renewal gaps against the production blocking window. Everything
  outside the shaded band is unreachable by the production blocker regardless of
  how good the comparison model is.}
  \label{fig:gapwindow}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Recall decomposes exactly.} End-to-end recall factorises as
\begin{equation}
R_{\mathrm{end\text{-}to\text{-}end}}
 = R_{\mathrm{blocking}} \times R_{\mathrm{scoring}\mid\mathrm{reachable}},
\end{equation}
and the framework asserts this identity numerically rather than assuming it, so
a probe's weakness is attributable to the stage that caused it. Pairs quality in
the production window is \rvPairsQualityProduction\ and the reduction ratio is
\rvReductionRatioProduction: the blocker discards almost all of the quadratic
comparison space, at the cost of also discarding most true matches.

\paragraph{The task is discriminative.} The overlap coefficient between the
score distributions of true matches and hard negatives is
\rvMatchNonmatchOverlap\ --- inside the gated band on both sides, so the task is
neither trivially separable nor hopeless. Mean reciprocal rank of the true match
among a source's candidates is \rvTrueMatchMrr, and the median top-1-to-top-2
margin is \rvMedianTopMargin: when the true match is present it usually ranks
first, but by a margin small enough that the threshold choice matters.

  The overlap of the two densities is what a comparison model must resolve; the
  vertical line marks the production acceptance threshold, shown for reference

\end{figure}

\input{\tabledir/probe_linkers}

Three frozen probe linkers are run --- a deterministic top-1 rule, a
score-threshold rule over all ranks, and a Fellegi--Sunter weight model
\citep{fellegi1969theory}. Their parameters were chosen once and never tuned
against these results, and none of them is the production threshold. Best pair
$F_1$ is \rvProbeBestFOne\ (\code{\rvProbeBestName}), comfortably inside the
gated band: a benchmark on which every probe scored near $1$ or near $0$ would
not discriminate between methods.

Note the divergence between pair-level and cluster-level scores: the
score-threshold probe attains pair $F_1 = \rvProbeThresholdFOne$ but B-cubed
$F_1 = \rvProbeThresholdBcubed$. Most notices are singletons, so cluster-level
metrics are dominated by correctly leaving them alone. Both are reported because
they answer different questions, and quoting only the flattering one would
mislead.

\section{Known limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Recurrence is unidentified.} Prevalence, gap distribution, text
  drift, and the specific CPV corruption mode are assumptions. Several distinct
  latent processes produce similar observed tables, so these parameters are
  partially identified at best.
  \item \textbf{Seed instability.} Difficulty metrics and probe rankings vary
  materially across seeds; see the previous section.
  \item \textbf{In-sample fidelity.} No real-BOAMP partition was held out from
  generator design, so the fidelity results are not out-of-sample
  \citep{snoke2018general}.
  \item \textbf{Residual metric failures.} \rvValidationMetricFailures\
  metric-level failures remain, all in non-critical gates; see
  Table~\ref{tab:discrepancies}.
  \item \textbf{Scope.} One region, one sector, two notice types. The residual
  \code{OTHER} notice type and the modification/cancellation lifecycle are not
  modelled.
  \item \textbf{Identity persistence.} Observed buyer identity is held constant
  within a notice family and, under the scoped block, across a need's cycles.
  This is an explicit reachability-versus-fidelity trade, not a neutral choice.
  \item \textbf{Placeholder taxonomy.} No manually validated technological
  taxonomy exists in the project, so needs use broad CPV-division segments.
  \item \textbf{Untested attack surfaces.} Membership-inference attacks
  \citep{vanbreugel2023membership}, neural-embedding semantic distance, and
  variance-based global sensitivity analysis are out of scope and are nowhere
  reported as passed.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Appropriate and inappropriate uses}

\input{\tabledir/readiness}

\paragraph{Supported now.} Debugging a linkage implementation end to end;
integration testing against a realistic schema; measuring how a method's
blocking recall responds to a known change in identifier availability;
demonstrating that a method degrades in the expected direction between the
\code{easier} and \code{stress} scenarios.

\paragraph{Not supported now.} Ranking two linkage methods; selecting an
acceptance threshold; quoting any precision or recall number as an estimate of
real-BOAMP performance; describing the benchmark as validated or released;
citing synthetic recurrence prevalence as evidence about real renewal behaviour.

\paragraph{Never supported.} Substituting for the real BOAMP ground truth. The
truth in this benchmark is generated. It is a correct answer to a constructed
question, and its usefulness rests entirely on the conditional claim in
Section~\ref{sec:objective} --- which the readiness assessment currently rates
as not yet established for comparison purposes.

\section{Conclusion}

The benchmark is internally correct and reproducible. Its latent world is
generated in a declared order from two independent seeds; its truth is a
deterministic function of realised cycle chains; no truth value, alias, or hash
reaches the observed table; and every one of the \rvReplayReplicatesChecked\
generated artifacts replays to identical canonical hashes under the pinned
environment.

Its observable realism is a measured, bounded claim rather than an assumption.
The candidate environment --- the property that decides whether linkage is
possible at all --- now matches the real corpus within the declared tolerances,
and the conditional observation mechanisms for identifiers, CPV, duration and
text reproduce the real corpus's structure rather than only its averages. Where
they do not, the residual is listed in Table~\ref{tab:discrepancies} rather than
smoothed away.

What remains unresolved is not fidelity but stability. Difficulty metrics and
probe rankings move materially between world seeds, and until they do not, the
benchmark cannot support controlled algorithm comparison or algorithm ranking.
That is the honest current position, and it is what the five-level readiness
assessment records.

Finally, the framing that should survive every use of this artifact: the
recurrence relation here is \emph{generated}, not discovered. It is not a
reconstruction of what French public buyers actually do, and it is not a
replacement for the BOAMP ground truth that does not exist. It is a controlled
instrument for measuring how a linkage method behaves when the answer is known,
under a set of assumptions this report has tried to make impossible to overlook.
