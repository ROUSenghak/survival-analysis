### 2c. Selection

**Frozen: `dur_w6`, the unchanged canonical duration-anchored ±6-month window.**

Explicit criteria, in the order applied:

1. *The synthetic recall deficit does not transfer.* The synthetic benchmark's low
   blocking recall at ±6m is caused by a verified generator defect: declared
   duration is sampled independently of the hidden cycle length, so the
   expected-end anchor carries no information (correlation −0.067 between declared
   duration and observed gap; declared duration median 6m against a true gap
   median of 15.1m). The window is mis-centred *in the benchmark*. That is not
   evidence that the real window is mis-centred, and widening the real window on
   the strength of it would import a synthetic artifact into the real analysis.
2. *Observable derivation.* The 6-month window is produced by a documented rule
   from an observable quantity (0.5 × median observed duration, floored at 6). The
   wider windows are not derived from anything; they are sensitivity settings.
3. *Precision cost is real and measurable.* Widening to ±18m raises the ambiguous
   POTENTIAL share from 32.6% to 40.8% and the candidate-cap rate from 0.15% to
   6.2%. Since no real labels exist, the added links cannot be shown to be correct.
4. *The link rate is not a target.* `dur_w12`/`dur_w18` land inside the internship
   guide's 40–60% band. That is explicitly not a selection criterion and was not
   used as one.

The three wider windows and the duration-free route are retained as the
pre-registered sensitivity set, and their survival consequences are reported in §6
as linkage uncertainty rather than resolved by choice.

## 3. Benchmark-supported real linkage strategy

`scripts/test_synthetic_to_real_transfer.py`. Real BOAMP has no renewal labels, so
a supervised linker can only reach it by transfer from synthetic training. The
current evidence shows material transfer risk concentrated in one feature:

| feature | synthetic mean | real mean | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **s_text** | 0.309 | 0.087 | **3.56** |
| s_cpv | 0.121 | 0.126 | 0.96 |
| s_time | 0.513 | 0.504 | 1.02 |
| s_buyer | 0.635 | 0.687 | 0.92 |
| gap_months | 5.94 | 7.65 | 0.78 |
| n_candidates_for_source | 10.2 | 10.9 | 0.94 |

The transfer script now mirrors the v0.4 benchmark model specification and uses
the frozen benchmark threshold table (`algorithm_thresholds.csv`). Applied to the
same real candidate pairs, gradient boosting accepts 411 rank-1 links (12.2%)
and logistic regression accepts 889 (26.3%), against 1,003 (29.7%) for the
transparent composite balanced rule. The models learned in a candidate
environment where the text signal is roughly 3.6× stronger than the real corpus
provides.

The labelled synthetic benchmark also now has the requested diagnostic metrics
and plots under
`reports/tables/synthetic_benchmark/v0_4_population_alias_revision/linkage_algorithm_benchmark/`
and
`reports/figures/synthetic_benchmark/v0_4_population_alias_revision/linkage_algorithm_benchmark/`:

`rank1_f1_threshold_curve.csv`, `gbm_loss_curve.csv`,
`diagnostic_curve_summary.csv`, `roc_curves.png`,
`precision_recall_curves.png`, `rank1_f1_threshold_curves.png`, and
`gbm_loss_curve.png`. These are benchmark-truth diagnostics only.

**Consequence: gradient boosting is now the provisional primary practical
real-linkage method under the frozen benchmark assumption.** More precisely, it
is the **best practical method under the predefined synthetic benchmark
assumptions and operational criteria**. This is not a claim that GBM is the real
truth or that its real precision/recall are known. It is a benchmark-supported
modelling choice: the synthetic benchmark was built to compare methods under
known hidden truth, GBM is the strongest held-out method, and there is no
labelled-real evidence that invalidates it. The transparent composite balanced
rule is retained as the auditable baseline, strict composite without POTENTIAL
links is retained as the conservative rule, broad composite is retained as
recall sensitivity, and logistic regression remains a supervised comparator.
Canonical strategy artifacts are:
`real_linkage_strategy_summary.csv`, `real_linkage_strategy_manifest.json`,
`real_primary_gbm_links.csv`, `real_linkage_method_agreement.csv`, and

## 5. The algorithm comparison cannot support a ranking

`scripts/cpv_anomaly_ablation.py`.

Synthetic CPV codes are invented 8-digit numbers behind a realistic division
prefix: ~39,300 distinct codes per world against 3,145 real, each reused 1.9 times
against 22.3 in reality, with only 11% of synthetic notices carrying a code that
exists anywhere in the real corpus. Exact codes do **not** enter blocking (blocking
is buyer key + time window); they enter only the `s_cpv` feature.

Because invented codes are near-unique, an exact CPV agreement in the synthetic
corpus almost only occurs between notices of the same latent need — it behaves as
a near-oracle feature. Degrading `s_cpv` to the division-level information that is
faithful gives:

| ablation | GBM | logistic | composite | rank-1 frequency (16 worlds) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| full CPV ladder | **0.269** | 0.210 | 0.150 | GBM 16/16 |
| division-only CPV | 0.154 | 0.155 | 0.150 | GBM 5, LR 6, composite 5 |

Under this ablation the ranking collapses to a three-way tie.

**Read this as an upper bound, not a measurement.** Collapsing `s_cpv` to
division-only removes *all* sub-division CPV agreement, including the part that
genuinely exists in real BOAMP — 4.70% of real Layer-1 candidate pairs share an
exact code (§ measured on `real_candidate_pairs_reproduced.csv`). So the ablation
strips real signal along with the artifact, and the resulting tie bounds how much
of gradient boosting's advantage *could* be artifact rather than showing how much
*is*. A controlled experiment that drew synthetic CPV codes from the empirical
BOAMP support, run on 2026-08-05, put the artifact's share of the margin at roughly
a quarter, with gradient boosting still leading; those artifacts were subsequently
removed at the maintainer's request and the measurement is recorded here as
provenance only, not as a citable result.

The defensible statement is therefore narrower than an earlier draft of this
document claimed: **the v0.4 algorithm comparison over-states the supervised
models' advantage by an amount bounded below by zero and above by the full margin,
and no algorithm ranking should be quoted from it without correcting CPV
generation.** It is not established that the whole advantage is artifact.

**This does not validate real BOAMP accuracy.** Supervised linkers remain
unlabelled on real BOAMP, and §3 shows a 3.56x `s_text` shift. The correct use is
GBM as the benchmark-supported primary practical rule, plus sensitivity analysis
and manual-audit prioritisation, not a real precision/recall claim.

### 6b. Linkage integrity (`linkage_integrity_checks.json`)
Clean: 0 self-links, 0 non-forward links, 0 non-positive gaps, 0 sources with more
than one accepted link. Flagged: **188 notices are accepted as the successor of
more than one source** (max 6), a many-to-one pattern the rank-1 rule permits and
does not resolve. Of 3,380 eligible sources, 2,005 have at least one candidate and
**1,375 (40.7%) have none** — these are censored by construction, not by evidence.
1,002 sources had a rank-1 candidate rejected below threshold; 327 accepted links
are ambiguous (POTENTIAL, margin < 0.05).

### 6c. Survival under the real-linkage strategy (`real_linkage_strategy_survival_headline.csv`)

| role | events | event rate | censoring | median | RMST(60m) | S(12m) | S(24m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **primary GBM** | 411 | 12.2% | 87.8% | not reached | **53.20** | 0.884 | 0.874 |
| transparent composite baseline | 1,003 | 29.7% | 70.3% | not reached | 43.51 | 0.721 | 0.691 |
| conservative composite | 422 | 12.5% | 87.5% | not reached | 52.93 | 0.879 | 0.871 |
| broad composite sensitivity | 1,504 | 44.5% | 55.5% | not reached | 35.43 | 0.589 | 0.537 |
| logistic sensitivity | 889 | 26.3% | 73.7% | not reached | 45.76 | 0.786 | 0.724 |

Median survival is not reached under any rule. RMST(60m) spans **35.4 to 53.2
months** across the strategy and sensitivity rules — the single largest source
of uncertainty in the analysis, and larger than any covariate effect below.

### 6d. Cox regression stability (`cox_hazard_ratio_stability.csv`)
Fitted separately under each rule (concordance 0.589–0.624).

Sign-stable across all three rules:

| covariate | HR (conservative → primary → baseline) |
|---|---|
| CPV division 72 (IT services) | 1.55 → 2.06 → 1.83 |
| CPV division 48 (software) | 1.57 → 1.56 → 1.50 |
| declared duration (months) | 0.947 → 0.930 → 0.928 |
| duration imputed | 0.731 → 0.653 → 0.717 |
| publication year | 0.978 → 0.951 → 0.955 |

**Not** sign-stable: `buyer_key_type_RAW_SIRET` (0.947 → 1.102 → 0.996), CPV
division 32, and the OTHER/missing CPV groups. Those directions are linkage
artifacts and must not be interpreted.

Subgroup Kaplan-Meier under the composite balanced baseline
(`survival_subgroups_by_rule.csv`): RAW_SIRET buyers renew faster than
NAME_FALLBACK buyers (event rate 32.3% vs 28.8%, RMST 41.77 vs 44.08, log-rank
p = 0.032), but the same contrast is **sign-unstable in Cox across the three
rules**, so the KM significance should not be reported as a finding. Imputed vs
observed duration is not significant (p = 0.407). `category_label` is degenerate
(the scope filter makes every source DIGITAL_ICT) and carries no information.

The duration and duration-imputed coefficients are sign-stable but remain
**uninterpretable causally**: declared duration defines the expected end date that
anchors candidate generation, so it is upstream of the event definition. This is
the pre-existing duration-leakage warning and it is unchanged.

### 6e. Linked-vs-unlinked bias (`linked_vs_unlinked_bias.csv`)
60 rows across publication year, buyer key type, CPV division, category, buyer
activity, CPV missingness, duration imputation and text-length quartile. Overall
link rate 29.7%. The event indicator is emphatically **not** missing at random:

*Buyer activity dominates everything else.*

| in-scope notices per buyer | n | link rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 398 | **0.0%** |
| 2–3 | 535 | 11.4% |
| 4–10 | 727 | 22.0% |
| 11+ | 1,720 | **45.5%** |

A buyer with a single in-scope contract can never produce a link, so 398 sources
are censored by arithmetic. Observed renewal is in large part a measure of buyer
publication volume.

*Publication year falls monotonically*, 37.2% (2015) to 13.1% (2026), because
recent contracts have less follow-up runway. **Any trend analysis over renewal
rates is confounded with administrative right-censoring and must be done on the
survival scale, not on raw link rates.**

Smaller gradients: RAW_SIRET 32.3% vs NAME_FALLBACK 28.8%; imputed duration 31.1%
vs observed 23.1%; CPV present 30.5% vs missing 24.4%.

## 7. Claims and limitations

**Supported.** The real Layer 1 candidate generator reproduces exactly and the
strategy thresholds are frozen. Under the benchmark-supported primary practical
GBM rule the observed-renewal event rate is 12.2% with 87.8% censoring and
RMST(60m) = 53.2 months. The transparent composite baseline remains available at
29.7% event rate for sensitivity and interpretation. The direction of the CPV-72,
CPV-48 and publication-year effects is stable across the three composite rules.

**Correction — the bit-exact replay claim does not currently verify.** The
recorded `replay_replicates.json` reports PASS for all 51 artifacts, but that was
recorded at generation time. Re-running `scripts/replay_synthetic_benchmark.py`
today gives `overall_status: FAIL` on `corruption_log` (291,855 recorded rows
against 293,136 replayed) and `observed_notices`. This was verified to be
**pre-existing environment drift, not caused by any change made today**: the
identical failure reproduces with all source changes reverted to `fbb4be2`.
`environment_drift_since_generation` reports NumPy 2.4.6 → 2.5.1 and pyarrow
24.0.0 → 25.0.0, and the README already documents that canonical hashing does not
survive a different NumPy build. Replay therefore needs the pinned
`requirements-lock.txt` environment to be re-verified; until then "all artifacts
replay" is a recorded historical result, not a currently reproducible one.

**Not supported.** Any real BOAMP precision or recall. Any real-BOAMP algorithm
ranking (§3 and §5). Any claim that the supervised transfer layers are correct or
incorrect without manual labels. Any causal reading of the duration coefficients.
Any claim that 12.2% or 29.7% is the true renewal prevalence — each is the output
of one decision rule among several that span 12.2%–44.5%.

**Gates that remain failed, and why that is correct.**
`READY_FOR_FINAL_ALGORITHM_RANKING` and
`READY_FOR_VALIDATED_SYNTHETIC_BENCHMARK_RELEASE` both remain FAIL. They should.
§5 shows the comparison behind them is confounded, and the cross-scenario
dispersion is genuine (end-to-end F1 0.069 in `stress` against 0.453 in `easier`
for the same model). `READY_FOR_CONTROLLED_ALGORITHM_COMPARISON` is currently
recorded as PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS. An earlier version of this document argued that
status was too generous; that argument is **withdrawn** — see the superseded note
in §5: the ablation bounds the artifact rather than measuring it, so it does not
by itself justify downgrading the gate. The status should be read with §5's
limitation attached — no ranking may be quoted until CPV generation is corrected.
