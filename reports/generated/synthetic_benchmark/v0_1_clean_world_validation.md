## 2. Pilot generation results (3 scenarios, `world_001/corruption_001`)

Seeds: `world_seed=20260721`, `corruption_seed=20260722` (benchmark_defaults_v0_1.yaml), `n_buyers=2000`.

| Scenario | Buyers | Establishments | Needs | Cycles | NEXT_CYCLE share | Notices (clean=observed row count) | Corruption log rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean_sanity | 2,000 | 2,174 | 4,893 | 12,794 | 61.8% | 17,708 | 3,683 |
| central_provisional | 2,000 | 2,174 | 4,893 | 6,914 | 29.2% | 9,537 | 32,710 |
| adverse_identity | 2,000 | 2,174 | 4,893 | 6,914 | 29.2% | 9,567 | 39,773 |

`clean_sanity`'s notice count (17,746) overshoots the ~10,000 pilot target
because its `base_recurrence_propensity=0.95` produces much longer cycle
chains than `central_provisional`/`adverse_identity` (`base_recurrence_propensity=0.40`)
— expected given the scenario's purpose (a near-noiseless positive control),
not a fidelity defect; `central_provisional` and `adverse_identity` land
within ~5% of the ~10,000-notice target.

Buyer/establishment/need/cycle row counts are unchanged from the original
pilot build for all 3 scenarios throughout this session. Notice counts have
shifted twice, both times deterministically and for documented reasons, not
run-to-run noise (confirmed by re-deriving `generation_metadata.json` fresh
from disk each time, never trusting an earlier written value):

1. `central_provisional` only: 9,534 -> 9,624, when
   `text.same_cycle_variation_severity` was recalibrated (0.15 -> 0.03, see
   `v0_1_fidelity_report.md` §0.3) — that parameter controls an early-exit
   branch in `apply_same_cycle_variation`, so changing it changes how many
   random draws are consumed per notice, shifting every subsequent draw in
   the shared `world_seed`-derived RNG stream (including, downstream, which
   needs end up with an AWARD notice).
2. **All 3 scenarios**, once `notices.py`'s `_schema_family` was changed
   from a hard Oct-2023 date cutoff to a probabilistic draw based on real
   by-year EFORMS-adoption share (`v0_1_fidelity_report.md` §0.4) — this
   adds a new `rng.random()` call for every notice dated 2024 or later,
   again shifting the downstream RNG stream: central_provisional
   9,624 -> 9,537, clean_sanity 17,746 -> 17,708, adverse_identity
   9,534 -> 9,567.

`clean_notices`' text *content* also changed slightly for all 3 scenarios
once `text_generation.render_text` was trimmed (a global template change),
independent of both count shifts above.
Corruption-log counts shift with each recalibration pass (duration,
identifier rates, then text/boilerplate). Every structural/referential
check in §1 was re-verified against the regenerated pilots after each
recalibration pass (79, then 79, then 124 tests passing as the check scope
widened to the full suite) and remains PASS throughout.

## 3. Adaptive calibration performed during this build (Phase 10)

**Buyer-activity concentration (Gini).** First pass used
`pareto_shape=1.10, offset=1.0` (chosen from the textbook asymptotic formula
Gini=1/(2·shape-1)≈0.83), but the *simulated* Gini at n=2,000 buyers was only
0.641 — the asymptotic formula is a poor guide at pilot scale, and the +1.0
location offset flattens the tail materially at this n. Empirically swept
`(shape, offset)` pairs at n=2,000 and re-measured Gini directly (the actual
check this parameter is calibrated against); `shape=1.0, offset=0.15`
produced Gini=0.814 across all three regenerated pilots (target 0.832 from
`calib_buyer_concentration_lorenz.csv`, tolerance ±0.15 per
`benchmark_defaults_v0_1.yaml`) — within tolerance and close numerically.
Fixed in `src/boamp/synthetic/buyers.py::generate_latent_buyers`; all three
pilots above reflect the corrected parameters.

## 4. Known v0.1 clean-world limitations (not defects, documented scope)

1. CPV sub-codes below the division level (6 trailing digits) are randomly
   generated per need, not drawn from any real detailed-CPV distribution —
   no such distribution exists at USE_DIRECTLY tier in this repo (v0.1
   placeholder taxonomy, per spec §4.3).
2. Duration profile (lognormal, mean 12 months) is a generator design
   choice, not calibrated to an observed duration-value distribution — only
   duration *presence/missingness* rates are OBSERVABLE-tier in this repo;
   no observed duration-value histogram was carried into
   `calibration_parameters_v0_1.yaml`.
3. Call-to-award delay (~N(90, 45) days, clipped) is a generator design
   choice; the real corpus's `call_to_award_delays.csv` is
   USE_AS_FIDELITY_TARGET, not consulted as a direct input in v0.1.
4. Buyer/establishment/need counts are Poisson-per-tier heuristics, not
   fit to the real corpus's exact needs-per-buyer distribution (no such
   distribution is observable — "needs" have no real-world analogue in
   BOAMP; only notices and buyers do).
5. `clean_sanity`'s overshoot vs the ~10,000-notice pilot target (§2) is
   accepted as scenario-appropriate, not corrected — narrowing it would mean
   lowering `base_recurrence_propensity` below what a "near-noiseless
   positive control" should be.

None of the above affect structural validity (§1); they are fidelity-tuning
opportunities for a future v0.2, tracked in the freeze gate
(`reports/tables/synthetic_benchmark/v0_1/benchmark_freeze_gate.csv`).
